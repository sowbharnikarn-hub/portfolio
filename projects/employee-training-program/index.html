Why the programme is built this way

Training that survives contact with real sprints

Most AI training is a webinar people forget by Friday. Three decisions separate this programme from that.

Specialise, don't generalise

An engineer and a design lead need different things from AI. Four role-based tracks — engineering, product and design, platform, leadership — each with its own curriculum, tools and seat allocation.

Learn on live work

Weeks 6–8 put the tools into real sprints on the company's own codebases, with Friday peer demos and a mid-programme checkpoint with managers. No sandbox exercises.

Baseline before you begin

Sprint velocity and review times are captured in weeks 1–2, before any AI tooling appears — so the +35% claim at day 90 is a comparison, not an assertion.
