The Pest Expert App

A demo Agile app-development project plan and product-launch marketing plan — built as a proposal for Carnot, an agri-tech company serving farmers. The goal: let a farmer report which pests and diseases are hitting which crops, across several fields, and get answers back in real time.

The Brief

Real-time answers, in a farmer's hand

Carnot wanted something deliberately simple: no dashboards to learn, no jargon. A farmer opens the app, says what's wrong and where, and gets a response fast enough to act on it that same day.

Who it serves

Farmers managing multiple crops — often spread across several farms or fields — who need to identify pests and diseases without specialist tooling.

What they asked for

A very simple mobile app: submit which pests or diseases are present in each crop, per field, and receive information back on what they're dealing with.

The non-negotiable

Responses had to be real time. A late answer is a useless answer — the whole point is taking action against the pest before the crop is lost.

I.

App Development Plan

An app of this scope runs roughly 6–9 months. The plan is staffed for that: a five-person development team with a dedicated tester, plus the design, product, support, sales and analysis roles that keep a build honest.

Product Launch Marketing Plan
